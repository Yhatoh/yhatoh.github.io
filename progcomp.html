ProgComp Topics and Excercices

Because of one specific team from UTFSM, I decided to create this page.

Here, I will sort problems into categories1.

This will be my completely subjective list of good problems to learn the

minimum knowledge necessary for teams participating in the ICPC.

Each exercise will have a number of *s in front of it to show

its difficulty.

Here is a rough definition of each difficulty:

  • *: If you have the required knowledge, this should be very easy.
  • **: You will need the required knowledge and some observations.
  • ***: You will need the required knowledge and some clever observations.
  • ****: Too hard to give a proper definition.

1. if a problem is in category doesn't mean that can't be solved using

other techniques.
